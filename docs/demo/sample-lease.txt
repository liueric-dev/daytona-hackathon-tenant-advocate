SAMPLE RESIDENTIAL LEASE (FICTIONAL - FOR DEMONSTRATION ONLY)

Landlord: Harborview Example Realty LLC (fictional). Tenant: Jordan Sample (fictional). Premises: Apartment 3F, 454 West 146 Street, New York, NY 10031.

1. TERM. This lease begins on November 1, 2026 and ends on October 31, 2027.

2. RENT. Tenant shall pay monthly rent of $2,450.00, due on the first day of each month. Rent received after the 5th day of the month is subject to a late fee of $150.00.

3. SECURITY DEPOSIT. Tenant shall deposit $4,900.00 as security, equal to two months' rent.

4. FEES. Tenant shall pay a non-refundable amenity fee of $250.00 per year and a key replacement fee of $75.00 per key.

5. REPAIRS. Tenant is responsible for all repairs to the apartment, including plumbing, heating equipment and appliances, regardless of cause.

6. HEAT AND HOT WATER. Landlord will supply heat and hot water as required by law.

7. ACCESS. Landlord may enter the apartment at any time without notice for any reason.

8. SUBLETTING. Tenant may not sublet or assign this lease without Landlord's prior written consent.

9. RENEWAL. This lease renews automatically for successive one-year terms at a rent increase of 8% unless Tenant gives notice 120 days before the end of the term.

10. EARLY TERMINATION. If Tenant leaves before the end of the term, Tenant owes all remaining rent for the full term.
